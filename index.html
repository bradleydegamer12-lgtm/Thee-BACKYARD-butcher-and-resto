The Yard

More Than
Just A Braai

Thee Backyard Butcher & Resto is where Protea Glen comes to eat. It starts with fresh meat and a hot fire, and it ends with people leaning back, talking too loud and going home full.

This is a proper South African shisa nyama — food prepared in front of you, served hot and made to be shared. No fuss, no shortcuts. Just big flavours, generous plates and that warm, relaxed atmosphere that makes a meal turn into a whole afternoon.
